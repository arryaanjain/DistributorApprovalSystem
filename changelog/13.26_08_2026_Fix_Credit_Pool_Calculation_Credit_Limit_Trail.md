# Implementation Plan: Fix Credit Pool Calculation, Distributor Active Status & Credit Limit Trail

Address the double-counting of credit limits on the Executive Dashboard, enforce proper `is_active` lifecycle status during distributor onboarding, remove hardcoded fallback limits, and provide a full credit decision history trail on the Distributors management page.

## User Review Required

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Key Lifecycle Policy**: Distributors will default to `is_active = false` during onboarding registration. The `is_active` flag will automatically flip to `true` when an admin approves their credit application or sanctions a credit limit in the `Applications.tsx` review panel.

> [!NOTE]
> **Credit Calculation Policy**: Dashboard credit totals and distributor list view limits will use `DISTINCT ON (distributor_id)` queries to select only the **latest active approved decision** for each distributor, ensuring historical credit adjustments do not inflate aggregate numbers.
