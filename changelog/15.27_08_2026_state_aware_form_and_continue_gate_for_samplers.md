# Implementation Plan: Gated Step Navigation & Trial-to-Credit Onboarding Flow

This plan establishes state-aware step gating in `ui/app/page.tsx` & `OnboardingStepNav.tsx`, ensuring distributors can only navigate to steps they have unlocked/completed. It also adds a seamless upgrade flow in `Step9Dashboard.tsx` allowing trial distributors to transition from sample kit evaluation to full credit onboarding.

## User Review Required

> [!IMPORTANT]
> - **Gated Navigation Enforcements**: Distributors will no longer be able to jump ahead to uncompleted steps (e.g. jumping directly from Step 1 to Step 7). Steps will unlock sequentially as backend status endpoints (`/onboarding/*`) confirm completion.
> - **Trial-to-Credit Transition**: Trial distributors who ordered sample kits will see a callout banner in `Step9Dashboard.tsx` reading *"If you are interested in commercial orders on credit, continue your onboarding"*, which directs them into Step 5 (KYC & GST) to complete their statutory and bank verification.
